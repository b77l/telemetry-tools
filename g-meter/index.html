Road profile: orient and secure the phone consistently. X/Y/Z are device axes until you align the phone with the vehicle.
Specific force (default) includes gravity: a stationary, level phone reads about 1 g in the gravity-aligned direction. Linear acceleration attempts to remove gravity. If the selected signal is unavailable, the recorder uses the available fallback and labels it in the CSV. Browser/device sensor capabilities vary.
