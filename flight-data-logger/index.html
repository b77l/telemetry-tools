Experimental phone telemetry only. Vehicle G assumes a rigid mount with phone top edge forward and screen facing up: device +Y maps forward, +X maps right, +Z maps up. The app uses gravity-free linear acceleration only; gravity-inclusive data are not shown as vehicle G. GPS groundspeed is not indicated airspeed. Phone-derived attitude, compass, GPS altitude and temperature are not certified instruments and must never be used for flight or vehicle safety decisions. Sensor support varies by device and browser.
